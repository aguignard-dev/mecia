What MECIA is — and is not.

MECIA is a lightweight research-analysis aid intended to remove repetitive spreadsheet work. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose disease, and does not replace experimental validation. Biological tissues can violate assumptions of simple linear, exponential and Hertz models; users remain responsible for method selection and interpretation.
